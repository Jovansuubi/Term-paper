Biography

I'm Suubi T Jovan Sserubo, a student at Bugema University studying BSE in Software Engineering. I grew up curious about how websites and apps work, and that curiosity turned into a course choice and then into a career goal.

Over the course of my studies I have moved from small class exercises to complete projects that I plan, design and build myself. I enjoy turning a vague idea into something people can actually use.

My professional interests are front-end development, accessible design and working in small teams that ship things.
